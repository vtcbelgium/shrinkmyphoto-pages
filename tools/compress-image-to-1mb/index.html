How to Compress Your Photo to 1024 KB

  1. Click Launch Compressor to load ShrinkMyPhoto with the 1024 KB preset preselected.
  2. Drag and drop your photo or click Choose photo (you can also paste directly with Ctrl+V).
  3. Review your target size (1024 KB) and chosen format.
  4. Click Shrink photo. The browser searches for the ideal quality setting in under 150ms.
  5. Click Download photo or Copy photo to use your newly compressed image immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why compress to 1MB?

Modern phone cameras shoot 12MP to 48MP photos weighing 8MB to 25MB. Compressing to 1MB allows instant transmission over mobile networks without noticeable visual loss.

Does this work offline?

Yes, because ShrinkMyPhoto runs 100% in your browser, once loaded it continues to compress photos even if you disconnect from the internet.
