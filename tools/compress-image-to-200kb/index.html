How to Compress Your Photo to 200 KB

  1. Click Launch Compressor to load ShrinkMyPhoto with the 200 KB preset preselected.
  2. Drag and drop your photo or click Choose photo (you can also paste directly with Ctrl+V).
  3. Review your target size (200 KB) and chosen format.
  4. Click Shrink photo. The browser searches for the ideal quality setting in under 150ms.
  5. Click Download photo or Copy photo to use your newly compressed image immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will this work for US Visa DS-160 photo upload?

Yes! The US State Department DS-160 photo portal requires JPEG files equal to or less than 240 KB. Compressing to 200KB guarantees instant acceptance without exceeding the limit.

Does compressing reduce the 600x600 px dimensions?

You can control dimension downscaling. If your photo is already 600x600 pixels, you can set resize to 'Original dimensions' so only byte compression is applied.

Are my visa photos stored anywhere?

Never. All image processing executes via local Canvas Web APIs inside your browser sandbox. Zero bytes are transmitted across the internet.
