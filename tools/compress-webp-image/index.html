How to Compress Your Photo to 80 KB

  1. Click Launch Compressor to load ShrinkMyPhoto with the 80 KB preset preselected.
  2. Drag and drop your photo or click Choose photo (you can also paste directly with Ctrl+V).
  3. Review your target size (80 KB) and chosen format.
  4. Click Shrink photo. The browser searches for the ideal quality setting in under 150ms.
  5. Click Download photo or Copy photo to use your newly compressed image immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why compress WebP instead of JPEG?

WebP offers superior lossy and lossless compression algorithms, typically achieving 25% to 35% smaller file sizes than JPEG at equivalent visual quality.

Does WebP support transparency?

Yes! WebP supports full alpha transparency while maintaining high compression ratios.
