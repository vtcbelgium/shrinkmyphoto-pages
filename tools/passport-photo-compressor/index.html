Frequently Asked Questions

What are the standard requirements for a digital passport photo?

Most embassies (including US, UK, EU, and Schengen) require JPEG format, between 600x600 and 1200x1200 pixels, with a file size strictly between 50KB and 240KB on a plain white background.

Can I replace the background with white?

ShrinkMyPhoto provides an automatic white background flattening option when converting transparent or cropped images to JPEG.
